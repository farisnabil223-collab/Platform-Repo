## 1. Monetization Expansion Roadmap (Sprint 24 Target)

- **Coupons & Promotional Codes:** Dynamic percentage/fixed discount codes with usage limit tracking (`Coupon` model).
- **Institutional Group Packages:** B2B seat purchasing for schools and universities (`GroupSubscription` model).
- **Automated Invoices & PDF Receipts:** Automated PDF invoice generation dispatches via email upon payment intent resolution.
- **Dunning & Subscription Recovery:** Grace period reminders and retry schedules for expired credit cards.
