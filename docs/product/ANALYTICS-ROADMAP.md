## 1. Product Analytics & Event Taxonomy (Sprint 29 Target)

- **User Lifecycle Events:** `student_registered`, `student_verified`, `login_success`, `password_reset_requested`.
- **Learning Engagement Events:** `course_viewed`, `course_enrolled`, `lesson_started`, `lesson_completed`, `quiz_attempted`, `exam_submitted`.
- **Monetization Events:** `checkout_started`, `payment_succeeded`, `subscription_activated`, `coupon_applied`.
- **Analytics Visualization:** Real-time Grafana and Admin Dashboard widgets for course completion rates, retention cohorts, and monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
