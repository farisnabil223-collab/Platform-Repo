## 1. Prioritization Scoring Methodology

Features are scored on a scale of 1 (Low) to 5 (High) across six dimensions:
- **User Impact (UI):** Value to Students, Teachers, and Parents.
- **Business Value (BV):** Strategic alignment and platform growth.
- **Revenue Impact (RI):** Direct monetization contribution.
- **Retention Impact (Ret):** Contribution to DAU/MAU retention.
- **Complexity (Comp):** Engineering effort (1 = Low complexity, 5 = High complexity).
- **Priority Score Formula:** `Score = (UI*2 + BV*2 + RI*2 + Ret*2) / Comp`
